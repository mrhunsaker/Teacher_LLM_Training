# Participant Lesson Handouts

These six handouts are designed to be used during the corresponding lessons in the Facilitator and Practice Edition. Participants should select the vision/educational or related-service case or a relevant profile from [Multidisciplinary Special Education Case Studies](multidisciplinary-case-studies.md) and adapt each handout to their professional role and scope of practice. They are text-first working pages. Participants may type into a copy, print them, or use them with a screen reader. Use fictional or district-approved information only.

## Handout 6: Copilot action report and capstone reflection

Use simulated messages for this activity. Do not connect a real mailbox during practice.

For each action item, record:

- **Task:** [Requested action.]
- **Source:** [Sender and subject.]
- **Due date:** [Exact stated date/time or "not stated / unclear".]
- **Suggested urgency (1–5):** [Rating.]
- **Rationale:** [Deadline, impact, and time sensitivity.]
- **Next step:** [Human action.]
- **Verification:** [What must be checked in the original message?]

**Capstone reflection**

- What did I ask the model to do?
- What did I explicitly prohibit?
- Which model suggestion did I verify, revise, or reject?
- What did the learner or colleague contribute?
- What evidence informed the final decision?
- What would I do differently next time?
- What district approval or support is needed before using this workflow in practice?

## Handout 7: AI output review — evidence, inference, unknown, error

For each important statement in an AI response, mark:

- **E — Evidence:** explicitly present in the prompt or verified source.
- **I — Inference:** a possible interpretation that requires review.
- **U — Unknown:** not established by available information.
- **X — Unsupported/error:** invented, contradicted, or stated with unjustified certainty.

**Statement from the AI response:**  
[Paste a fictional practice statement.]

**My label and reason:**  
[Explain the evidence or missing evidence.]

**What I would verify or ask next:**  
[Record the source, observation, calculation, or question.]

**Disposition:** retain / revise / reject / gather more evidence.

## Handout 8: Six-step critical review record

1. **Scope:** Did the response stay within the task and stop condition?
2. **Evidence:** Which statements are supported by supplied facts or verified sources?
3. **Accuracy:** Did I check calculations, dates, citations, product details, and policy claims?
4. **Fit:** Does it fit the learner, task, setting, access needs, and purpose?
5. **Accessibility and privacy:** Is the artifact usable and was information handled appropriately?
6. **Decision:** What will I retain, revise, reject, or investigate further?
