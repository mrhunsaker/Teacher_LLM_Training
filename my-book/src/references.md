# References and Further Learning

These resources are starting points. Verify current versions and district/state requirements before applying them to practice.

## Expanded Core Curriculum and visual impairment

- [Perkins School for the Blind: Expanded Core Curriculum](https://www.perkins.org/resource/expanded-core-curriculum/) — overview and resources related to the ECC.
- [American Foundation for the Blind](https://www.afb.org/) — resources for educators, families, and professionals supporting people who are blind or have low vision.

## IEP requirements

- [IDEA regulation: 34 CFR § 300.320 — Definition of individualized education program](https://sites.ed.gov/idea/regs/b/d/300.320) — includes present levels, measurable annual goals, progress measurement, and progress reporting requirements.
- [IDEA regulations, Part B](https://sites.ed.gov/idea/regs/b) — federal regulatory text. Consult state and district guidance for implementation.

## Microsoft Copilot and Outlook

- [Enterprise data protection in Microsoft Copilot and Microsoft Copilot Chat](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/enterprise-data-protection) — explains enterprise data protection commitments for eligible work/school experiences.
- [Copilot Chat in Microsoft 365 apps](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/how-copilot-chat-works-in-microsoft-365-apps) — describes differences in capabilities based on license and app.
- [Prioritize my inbox](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/outlook/copilot-outlook/prioritize-my-inbox) — availability, setup, and limitations of Outlook's built-in prioritization feature.
- [Summarize an email thread with Copilot in Outlook](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/outlook/copilot-pages/summarize-an-email-thread-with-copilot-in-outlook) — steps for summarizing a thread.

## Privacy and implementation

- [U.S. Department of Education: FERPA](https://studentprivacy.ed.gov/ferpa) — federal student privacy information.
- Consult district policy, approved software lists, data governance procedures, and your district privacy/technology office before using AI with education records.
