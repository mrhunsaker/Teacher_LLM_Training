# Unit 2: AI literacy and independent performance

Unit 2 builds the critical AI literacy and independent judgment needed to evaluate AI-assisted work. Across Sessions 7–10, you will examine how LLMs work and fail, challenge common misconceptions, verify claims and calculations, test accessibility and privacy, and demonstrate a complete bounded workflow in an independent capstone.

Continue using the same fictional case selected for Unit 1. Complete the materials in order and keep evidence of what you verified, revised, rejected, and decided independently.

## Unit materials

### [2.2 AI Literacy Self-Directed Practice Guide — Sessions 7–10](ai-literacy-self-study-guide.md)

The guide organizes the final four sessions into an independent study sequence. It provides learning activities, practice tasks, and evidence expectations for building AI literacy and preparing for the capstone.

### [2.3 Lesson 7 — AI Literacy Foundations](09-ai-literacy-foundations.md)

Establish a practical understanding of generative AI and LLM behavior, including fluent but fallible output, variation, uncertainty, and the need to match tools to tasks. Identify appropriate uses and situations where not using AI is the better decision.

### [2.4 Lesson 8 — AI Misconceptions and Failure Analysis](10-misconceptions-and-failure-analysis.md)

Analyze common misconceptions and deliberately flawed AI responses. Practice separating supplied facts, hypotheses, and unknowns; identify hallucinations and unsupported inferences; and determine what evidence or professional review is needed.

### [2.5 Lesson 9 — Critical Evaluation Lab](11-critical-evaluation-lab.md)

Apply a repeatable review sequence covering scope, evidence, accuracy, fit, accessibility, privacy, and final disposition. Audit goal drafts, data calculations, sources, and generated materials, then document whether to retain, revise, reject, or investigate further.

### [2.6 Lesson 10 — Independent Performance Capstone](12-independent-performance-capstone.md)

Demonstrate the complete Plan → Refine → Build → Refine process using a fictional, role-relevant scenario. Produce a bounded prompt and practical artifact, inspect a flawed AI response, verify evidence and calculations, address privacy and accessibility, and explain which decisions remain with qualified professionals and the IEP team.

### [2.7 AI Literacy and Misconception Assessment](ai-literacy-assessment.md)

Use the parallel pre- and post-assessment items to evaluate critical reasoning about citations, generalization, data interpretation, privacy, accessibility, and professional boundaries. Interpret this assessment separately from the applied-course assessment, competency checklist, and capstone rubric.

## Unit 2 checkpoint

Before completing the course, confirm that you can identify unsupported AI claims, verify important statements against authoritative sources or raw data, recognize when context is insufficient, test accessibility and privacy safeguards, and explain why professional judgment remains with the educator and authorized team.
