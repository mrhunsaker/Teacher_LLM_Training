# Participant Pre- and Post-Assessment

Use the editable Word document **`participant-pre-and-post-assessment.docx`** for this course activity. This page is a download and use guide; the full participant material is maintained in the DOCX file rather than reproduced as Markdown here.

## Download

[Download participant-pre-and-post-assessment.docx](https://github.com/mrhunsaker/Teacher_LLM_Training/raw/refs/heads/main/my-book/handouts/docx/participant-pre-and-post-assessment.docx)

Parallel pre- and post-course assessment forms, participant directions, scoring guide, and interpretation guidance.

Open the DOCX in Microsoft Word or a compatible editor. Save a working copy before entering responses or recording review results.

## Participant use

- Complete the pre-assessment before Session 1 and the parallel post-assessment after the Session 10 capstone.
- Respond independently and explain your reasoning. If information is missing, identify what is unknown rather than guessing.
- Use fictional information only; do not enter student records into an AI tool for this assessment.
- Use the scoring guide in the DOCX. Interpret pre/post differences as evidence on these scenarios, not proof of transfer or a credential.

## Source and privacy

The DOCX is generated from a Markdown source maintained in the repository for course-authoring and regeneration. Participants should use the DOCX rather than the source Markdown. Use fictional or district-approved information only, and do not enter identifiable student or staff information into an unapproved system.
