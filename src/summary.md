# Course Overview

This course equips teachers of students with visual impairments and their classroom partners to use LLMs and Microsoft 365 Copilot in carefully bounded, privacy-conscious ways while teaching the Expanded Core Curriculum (ECC). The course is designed for educators who are new to AI and spreadsheet-based progress monitoring.

## The Expanded Core Curriculum

The ECC supplements the general education curriculum with disability-specific skills that students with visual impairments may need to access information, participate meaningfully, and build independence. The nine traditional areas are compensatory or functional academic skills (including communication modes), orientation and mobility, social interaction skills, independent living skills, recreation and leisure skills, career education, use of assistive technology, sensory efficiency skills, and self-determination. Instruction is individualized; not every student needs the same instruction in every area.

## Course Outcomes

By the end of the course, participants will be able to:

- Write a constrained prompt that specifies the task, inputs, limits, output format, and stop condition.
- Use fictional or district-approved de-identified baseline data to ask an LLM for possible IEP goal and objective language, while retaining educator and IEP-team responsibility.
- Design observable measures and aligned data sheets for a TVI and classroom teacher.
- Enter repeated measures in Excel, graph performance, and apply an explicit criterion rule such as greater than 90% on at least three distinct sessions.
- Examine level, trend, variability, prompt dependence, and generalization before describing progress or a possible plateau.
- Use Microsoft 365 Copilot in Outlook, where licensed and enabled, to summarize email action items, due dates, and a 1–5 suggested urgency rating for human review.

## Course Sequence

## Module 1 — Prompting

Participants learn a repeatable prompt structure: role, task, context, constraints, output format, and stop condition. They practice limiting scope and asking the model to identify missing information rather than inventing it.

## Module 2 — IEP Goal Brainstorming

Participants prepare a concise de-identified learner profile with baseline data and instructional context. They use an LLM to brainstorm several measurable goal options and objectives, then check alignment to the ECC, present levels, measurement conditions, and individualized criteria.

## Module 3 — Data Collection

Participants operationalize the target skill and build two aligned collection forms: one for the TVI and one for the classroom teacher. The forms capture date, setting, opportunities, correct responses, prompt level, accommodations, and notes needed to interpret performance.

## Module 4 — Excel Monitoring

Participants use a structured data table, formulas, a line chart, and a transparent criterion rule. The requested training convention is greater than 90% on at least three separate qualifying sessions. This is a configurable instructional rule, not a universal IEP requirement or a legal standard.

## Module 5 — Data-Based Decisions

Participants distinguish a data pattern from an instructional conclusion. They inspect the number and quality of data points, trend, variability, instructional fidelity, access barriers, and performance across people and settings before considering a plateau or changing instruction.

## Module 6 — Copilot Email Triage

Participants use district-provided Microsoft 365 Copilot capabilities to review the Outlook inbox, extract action items and dates, and apply a suggested urgency scale. They configure important senders and recipients using role descriptions or approved contacts, check original messages, and keep the final decisions under human control.

## Assessment and Evidence of Learning

Participants complete a fictional case study that includes a bounded prompt, a goal brainstorm, paired collection sheets, an Excel graph and criterion check, a brief data interpretation, and a Copilot-style daily action list. A peer or facilitator checks each product against the quality checks in the relevant module.

## Important Implementation Note

AI output and spreadsheet calculations are aids, not authorities. IEP goals must be individualized and team-developed. The IDEA regulation requires measurable annual goals and a description of how progress toward goals will be measured and when periodic progress reports will be provided. See [34 CFR § 300.320](https://sites.ed.gov/idea/regs/b/d/300.320). District policy, applicable privacy requirements, and professional standards govern use of student information.
