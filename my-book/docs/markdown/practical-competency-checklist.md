# Practical Competency Checklist

Use the editable Word document **`practical-competency-checklist.docx`** for competency self-review during practice and the capstone. This page is a use guide and download link; the checklist items and rating fields are maintained in the DOCX rather than reproduced as Markdown.

## Download the checklist

[Download practical-competency-checklist.docx](https://github.com/mrhunsaker/Teacher_LLM_Training/raw/refs/heads/main/my-book/handouts/docx/practical-competency-checklist.docx)

Open the DOCX in Microsoft Word or a compatible editor and save one working copy per observation or review period. The document contains the rating scale, ten competency areas, evidence fields, summary record, and suggested readiness interpretation.

## How to use it

1. Enter the participant, reviewer (if applicable), date, selected case and professional role, and task or artifact reviewed.
2. Rate each observable behavior separately: **0** (not demonstrated/unsafe), **1** (emerging), **2** (demonstrated), or **N/O** (not observed).
3. Record a brief, specific evidence example for every rating. A rating without evidence is incomplete.
4. Do not score N/O as zero. Explain when an item is not relevant or there was no valid opportunity to observe it.
5. Complete the area summaries and identify the strongest demonstrated behaviors, one priority for further practice, and a next review point.

The checklist is formative. It is not a credential, employment evaluation, legal standard, or substitute for district approval. Optional peer review may supplement, but does not replace, evidence-based self-review.

## Relationship to the capstone

The checklist uses an item-level **0–2** scale for formative review. The independent capstone uses a separate **0–3 domain-level** performance rubric. Do not add the scores or convert one scale into the other. Use the checklist to identify behaviors to practice; use the capstone rubric to review the independent demonstration under the stated scenario.

## Source and privacy

The DOCX is generated from the course-authoring Markdown source. Participants should use the DOCX, not the source Markdown. Use fictional or district-approved information only. Do not enter identifiable student or staff information into an unapproved system. Reserved professional decisions remain with qualified professionals and authorized teams.
