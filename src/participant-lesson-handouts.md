# Participant Lesson Handouts

Use this packet as a working record across the ten-session course. Select **one role-relevant case** from the [unified Buendía-family case collection](multidisciplinary-case-studies.md) and carry it through the activities. All ten cases are equal options. Adapt each task to your professional role and scope of practice.

These handouts are text-first and can be completed in a copy, printed, or used with a screen reader. Use fictional or district-approved information only. Do not enter identifiable student or staff information into an unapproved system.

## Handout 8 — Six-step critical review record

**Purpose:** Apply a consistent review before accepting or sharing an AI-assisted artifact.

### Review the response

1. **Scope:** Did the response stay within the task and stop condition?
2. **Evidence:** Which statements are supported by supplied facts or verified sources?
3. **Accuracy:** Did I check calculations, dates, citations, product details, and policy claims?
4. **Fit:** Does it fit the learner, task, setting, access needs, and purpose?
5. **Accessibility and privacy:** Is the artifact usable, and was information handled appropriately?
6. **Decision:** What will I retain, revise, reject, or investigate further?
