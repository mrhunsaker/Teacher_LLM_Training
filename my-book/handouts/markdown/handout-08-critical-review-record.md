# Handout 7 — AI-output review: evidence, inference, unknown, error

**Purpose:** Classify important statements in an AI response before relying on them.

### Use the classification key

- **E — Evidence:** Explicitly present in the prompt or a verified source.
- **I — Inference:** A possible interpretation that requires review.
- **U — Unknown:** Not established by available information.
- **X — Unsupported or error:** Invented, contradicted, or stated with unjustified certainty.
