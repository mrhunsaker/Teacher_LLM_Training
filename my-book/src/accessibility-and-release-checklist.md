The release-quality checklist for build and link checks, navigation, screen-reader review, discipline-specific access, and release sign-off.

Open the DOCX in Microsoft Word or a compatible editor. Save a working copy before entering responses or recording review results.

## Participant use

- Use the checklist on the rendered mdBook before a release. A successful build alone does not establish accessibility.
- Record the actual browser, operating system, screen reader/access technology, and test outcomes.
- Mark an item complete only after someone has performed the check. Record issues, corrections, limitations, and the release decision.
- Do not claim accessibility conformance or certification from this practical checklist alone.
